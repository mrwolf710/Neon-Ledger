# Story bible: what is actually happening (agreed with the owner)

Hale is a woman (she/her).

## The truth
The Civic Audit Bureau runs an **off-book program**: it lends memory auditors to the highest bidder as deniable killers, then **wipes the auditor's memory** of the job so nobody can testify. Juno Vale is the program's best tool. **Hale is her handler** and has run her for years (the lost hours on Line 9, "I don't need a repeat of last time").

- **Juno killed Dex, on Hale's order**, then was wiped (the 47-minute gap, 23:10-23:57). The override code and the grey glove are hers. She cannot remember it.
- Dex's hidden echo shows a woman in a grey coat (Juno) reaching for a **dying man's implant**: that man is an **earlier victim** (months ago), not Dex. Dex held proof of the program's first kill and died trying to sell it.
- The **corporate fixer** works for a corporation that is a client of the program. He wants the echo to **blackmail the Bureau**, not to expose it. His deal ("your forty-seven minutes never happened, the case closes on a burglar") is a lie that keeps Juno useful to him.
- Kit saw the auditor leave; Mama Teo, the vendors and Kit are bystanders the program does not care about, and who pay for it.

## New from the owner (supersedes the ending below where they differ)
- **The fixer offers to restore Juno's memories.** They include **many, many assassinations for the Bureau**. It is devastating: her mind has been wiped over and over and over again. (Proposed logic: the fixer's corporation, as a client, **kept copies of every wipe** as insurance, which is the real blackmail material and why his offer is credible.)
- **The finale: Hale shows up and tries to shoot Juno.** **Kit and Mama Teo work together to take Hale out and save Juno.** (Needs setup earlier: why Teo and Kit are there, and a chance for each to matter.)

## Decided finale (owner, revised)
(The first version had Kit shouting and Teo swinging a cleaver; the game has no audible dialogue and no attack animations, so it changed.)
1. **The restoration is a playable memory montage:** a short run of echo scenes Juno watches, each a kill in her own hands, faster and faster. She learns she has been wiped over and over and over.
2. **Juno goes back to Lowmarket to get Kit's and Teo's help.** She asks them because she knows Hale will come for her.
3. **Kit's rare cyber gear disables Hale.** Kit is a collector of rare cyber gear (that is what he spends the bribe money on). He has what is needed (e.g. an override damper / dead-man jammer that shuts down Hale's weapon and implant). No dialogue shouting, no combat animation: Kit uses the gear, Hale goes down. Teo is there with Kit.
4. **The showdown is on the empty Line 9 platform** where the game began. Hale steps out of the train and tries to shoot Juno; Kit's gear stops her.
5. **Then Juno wipes herself anyway.** She cannot live with hundreds of kills even though she was saved; Teo and Kit watch. The game closes on the opening line: a train arrives and Juno says "I think I lost an hour on that train."

### Setup the earlier chapters must plant
- **Juno's first conversation with Mama Teo:** they know and like each other, and Teo hints she wishes Juno would get out of the auditing business because it is bad for Juno. (Draft in this file's history; being agreed with the owner.) This gives Teo the desire to help and makes the sad ending hurt more.
- **Kit collects rare cyber gear:** shown in the hostel (an examinable shelf of gear; a line when Juno has paid him: the credits go to the collection). This gives him the ability to disable Hale.
- The Bureau scenes must make Hale believably dangerous and personally invested.
- The fixer's corporation has kept a copy of every wipe: the source of the montage and of his leverage.

## Ending (earlier choice C, now confirmed as the last beat above)
Juno destroys the proof and her own memory together, to protect Teo, Kit and the others. Hale falls or does not (to be decided), but Juno is back where she started. The game ends on the opening: a train arrives and Juno says "I think I lost an hour on that train." (Bleak, closes the loop.)

## Chapter plan (5 more locations before the end; the Bureau is built)
1. **The Bureau (built): interrogation and the memory-audit lab.** Hale tries to close it; the lab shows a wipe signature in Juno's own implant.
2. **Lowmarket at dawn** (a visit back; rain stopped). The Sable, Teo, Kit; someone saw her leave Dex's room; a clue on the Line 9 platform ties her lost hours to Hale.
3. **An arcade / casino / pachinko parlor** (owner's addition, to show off the graphics): a quick scene. Juno walks in, talks to **one intermediary** who points her to where she must go next (proposed: the fixer's go-between, who tells her where the fixer will meet her). Loud, colourful, packed with flashing machines, in strong contrast to the dark empty Bureau.
4. **The fixer's place, a corporate tower.** His real offer: her memory back in exchange for the echo.
5. **The empty Line 9 platform** where the game began: the ending.
